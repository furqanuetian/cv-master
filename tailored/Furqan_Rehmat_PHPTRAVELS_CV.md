**Strategy:** Facilitated emergency alignment between engineering and client stakeholders. Adopted a reverse merge strategy to safely resolve legacy issues without altering production code. Maintained live risk logs and structured client communication throughout.
**Result:** Avoided an estimated 15-day schedule slippage, delivered 20+ technical packages on time, and protected a critical compliance milestone.

### Holding the Line on Requirements
**Challenge:** A US client pushed to start development on incomplete requirements, risking significant rework and safety compliance issues.
**Strategy:** Presented a data-backed gap analysis showing critical unknowns — undefined communication protocols and missing payload specifications — and walked the client through 3 specific test-failure scenarios to secure formal sign-off before development proceeded.
**Result:** Prevented 2 weeks of inefficient rework. Ensured compliance with safety standards. Maintained the project schedule.

### Closing a Requirements Gap with Low-Fidelity Prototypes
**Challenge:** A US client's requirements for a dashboard UI were ambiguous, risking misaligned development, significant rework, and delayed delivery.
**Strategy:** Created low-fidelity Figma prototypes to visualise key UI states and user flows. Facilitated structured design-review sessions with the client to validate interactions and obtain formal sign-off before development began.
**Result:** Achieved client sign-off in days rather than weeks. Significantly reduced UI rework and accelerated frontend development.

### Diagnosing a Hardware-Firmware Mismatch
**Challenge:** NFC integration failures stalled the HiL test suite for three weeks due to a mismatch between legacy hardware and updated firmware protocols.
**Strategy:** Led a cross-team investigation, facilitated deep-dive sessions with developers and client technical leads, and identified the root cause.
**Result:** Successfully unblocked the NFC integration. Coordinated procurement of upgraded hardware. Delivered a clean code commit to Bitbucket.
