### Diagnosing a Hardware-Firmware Mismatch
**Challenge:** NFC integration failures stalled the HiL test suite for three weeks due to a mismatch between legacy hardware and updated firmware protocols — a critical blocker for the industrial client's manufacturing validation timeline.
**Strategy:** Led a cross-team investigation, facilitated deep-dive sessions with developers and client technical leads at Industrial Scientific Corporation, and identified the hardware-version mismatch as root cause.
**Result:** Successfully unblocked the NFC integration. Coordinated procurement of upgraded hardware (Unit Version 3). Delivered a clean code commit to Bitbucket and established a hardware-software version verification process to prevent recurrence.

### Unblocking a Stalled QA Pipeline
**Challenge:** The automated V&V test suite had fallen behind the main development branch, threatening a critical Q3 compliance milestone for a US-based industrial client.
**Strategy:** Facilitated emergency alignment between engineering and client stakeholders across time zones. Adopted a reverse merge strategy to safely resolve legacy issues without altering production code.
**Result:** Avoided an estimated 15-day schedule slippage, delivered 20+ technical packages on time, and protected a critical compliance milestone.

### Holding the Line on Requirements
**Challenge:** A US client pushed to start development on incomplete requirements, risking significant rework and safety compliance issues.
**Strategy:** Presented a data-backed gap analysis showing critical unknowns — undefined communication protocols and missing payload specifications — and walked the client through 3 specific test-failure scenarios to secure formal sign-off before development proceeded.
**Result:** Prevented 2 weeks of inefficient rework. Ensured compliance with safety standards. Maintained the project schedule.

### Closing a Requirements Gap with Low-Fidelity Prototypes
**Challenge:** A US client's requirements for a dashboard UI were ambiguous, risking misaligned development and costly rework.
**Strategy:** Created low-fidelity Figma prototypes to visualise key UI states and user flows. Facilitated structured remote design-review sessions to validate interactions and obtain client sign-off before development began.
**Result:** Achieved client sign-off in days rather than weeks. Significantly reduced UI rework and accelerated frontend development.
