### Closing a Requirements Gap with Low-Fidelity Prototypes
**Challenge:** A US client's requirements for a dashboard UI were ambiguous, risking misaligned development and costly rework.
**Strategy:** Created low-fidelity Figma prototypes to visualise key UI states and user flows. Facilitated structured design-review sessions to validate interactions and obtain client sign-off before development began.
**Result:** Achieved client sign-off in days rather than weeks. Significantly reduced UI rework and accelerated frontend development.

**Challenge:** A US client pushed to start development on incomplete requirements, risking significant rework and compliance issues.
**Strategy:** Presented a data-backed gap analysis showing critical unknowns and walked the client through 3 specific test-failure scenarios to secure formal sign-off before development proceeded.
**Result:** Prevented 2 weeks of inefficient rework. Ensured compliance with safety standards. Maintained the project schedule.

**Challenge:** NFC integration failures stalled the HiL test suite for three weeks due to a mismatch between legacy hardware and updated firmware protocols.
**Strategy:** Led a cross-team investigation, facilitated deep-dive sessions with developers and client technical leads, and identified the root cause.
